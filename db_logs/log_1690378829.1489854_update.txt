When feeling overwhelmed due to a heavy workload, there are several strategies that can help:

1. Prioritize: Identify the most important tasks and focus on those first. It's okay if you can't get to everything.

2. Take Breaks: Regular rest periods can help refresh and refocus the mind.

3. Exercise: Physical activity can reduce feelings of fatigue and improve mood.

4. Healthy Eating: A balanced diet can provide more energy and improve overall well-being.

5. Sleep: Adequate sleep is crucial as lack of it can significantly contribute to feelings of fatigue.

6. Seek Support: Sharing feelings with a trusted individual can make a big difference. If comfortable, consider speaking with a professional, such as a counselor or therapist, who can provide strategies and tools to help manage stress and workload.

7. Ask for Help: It's okay to ask for help when you need it. This can be a significant step in managing your workload and stress.
