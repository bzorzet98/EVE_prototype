I'm sorry to hear that you're feeling this way, Bruno. It's completely normal to feel overwhelmed when you have a lot on your plate. Here are a few suggestions that might help:

1. Prioritize: Try to identify what tasks are most important and focus on those first. It's okay if you can't get to everything.

2. Take Breaks: It's important to give your mind and body rest. Even short breaks can help you feel more refreshed and focused.

3. Exercise: Physical activity can help reduce feelings of fatigue and improve your mood.

4. Healthy Eating: Make sure you're eating a balanced diet. Proper nutrition can give you more energy and help you feel better overall.

5. Sleep: Ensure you're getting enough sleep. Lack of sleep can significantly contribute to feelings of fatigue.

6. Seek Support: Talk to someone you trust about what you're going through. Sometimes, just sharing your feelings can make a big difference.

Remember, it's okay to ask for help when you need it. If you feel comfortable, you might also consider speaking with a professional, such as a counselor or therapist. They can provide strategies and tools to help manage stress and workload.
